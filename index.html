About

I'm Nicolás León, a screen composer based in Bogotá specializing in atmospheric and narrative-driven scores. My work spans animated short films, video games, trailers, and live exhibitions — always built around the idea that music should be felt before it's heard.

My compositions have been part of projects recognized at FICCI, Santiago Horror Festival, Bakunawa Fest, Bogoshorts, and the Cinemateca de Bogotá.
